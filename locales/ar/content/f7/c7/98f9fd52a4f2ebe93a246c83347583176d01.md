- [Go Resources](http://golang.org/help)
- [StackOverflow](http://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/go)
- [Awesome Go](https://github.com/avelino/awesome-go): [E-Books](https://github.com/avelino/awesome-go#e-books) و[Tutorials](https://github.com/avelino/awesome-go#tutorials)
