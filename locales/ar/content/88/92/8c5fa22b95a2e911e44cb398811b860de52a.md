# التعليمات

أنشد قافية الأطفال «This is the House that Jack Built».

> إن عملية وضع عبارة أو جملة داخل عبارة أو جملة أخرى تُسمّى التضمين.
> وبالعودية والتضمين نستطيع أن نأخذ عددًا محدودًا من الصيغ (الكلمات والعبارات) ونبني عددًا لا نهائيًا من التعبيرات.
> كما يتيح لنا التضمين بناء بنية لا نهائية الطول، نظريًا على الأقل.

- [papyr.com][papyr]

وهذه القافية كما يلي:

This is the house that Jack built.

This is the malt
that lay in the house that Jack built.

This is the rat
that ate the malt
that lay in the house that Jack built.

This is the cat
that killed the rat
that ate the malt
that lay in the house that Jack built.

This is the dog
that worried the cat
that killed the rat
that ate the malt
that lay in the house that Jack built.

that tossed the dog
that worried the cat

that lay in the house that Jack built.

that worried the cat

that lay in the house that Jack built.

that ate the malt

This is the priest all shaven and shorn

that tossed the dog

that ate the malt

that worried the cat

that lay in the house that Jack built.

This is the farmer sowing his corn

This is the horse and the hound and the horn

that kissed the maiden all forlorn
that milked the cow with the crumpled horn
that tossed the dog
that worried the cat
that killed the rat
that ate the malt
that lay in the house that Jack built.
