# 关于

C# 内置了常见数学运算的运算符：

- [`+`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#addition-operator-)：加法
- [`-`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#subtraction-operator--)：减法
- [`/`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#division-operator-)：除法
- [`*`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#multiplication-operator-)：乘法
- [`%`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#remainder-operator-)：求余

还有两个特殊的运算符：

- [`++`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#increment-operator-)：自增
- [`--`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#decrement-operator---)：自减

这两个运算符分别用于把值加 1 或减 1。
