# 简介

Factor 中的字符串是字符组成的序列。`sequences`词汇表中的大多数词都能作用于字符串，此外还有[`splitting`][splitting]、[`ascii`][ascii]等词汇表中专门处理字符串的词。

```factor
USING: ascii sequences splitting ;

"hello, world" .                  ! => "hello, world"
"foo: bar" ": " split1 .s
! => "foo"
! => "bar"
"WARNING" >lower .                ! => "warning"
"  spaced  " [ blank? ] trim .    ! => "spaced"
"warning" "(" ")" surround .      ! => "(warning)"
```

`split1`和`split`（位于[`splitting`][splitting]词汇表中）用于切分字符串：`split1`在分隔符第一次出现的位置切分，`split`则按一组字符中的任意一个字符来切分。`surround`和`glue`（位于[`sequences`][sequences]词汇表中）用于构建字符串：`surround`给字符串加上前缀和后缀，`glue`则用分隔符连接两个字符串。[关于](./about.md)页面列出了每个字符串处理词以及它所在的词汇表。

[splitting]: https://docs.factorcode.org/content/vocab-splitting.html
[sequences]: https://docs.factorcode.org/content/vocab-sequences.html
[ascii]: https://docs.factorcode.org/content/vocab-ascii.html
