# 资源

## 学习

- [Nim basics](https://narimiran.github.io/nim-basics/)：适合编程初学者。
- [Nim tutorial, part 1](https://nim-lang.org/docs/tut1.html)：适合有一定编程经验的人。
- [Learn X in Y minutes for Nim](https://learnxinyminutes.com/docs/nim/)：语法概览。
- [Nim for Python programmers](https://github.com/nim-lang/Nim/wiki/Nim-for-Python-Programmers)
- [Nim for C programmers](https://github.com/nim-lang/Nim/wiki/Nim-for-C-programmers)
- [Nim in Action](https://www.manning.com/books/nim-in-action)：有纸质书和电子书两种版本。

更多内容请见官方的 [Learn Nim](https://nim-lang.org/learn.html) 页面。

- [Searchable index](https://nim-lang.org/docs/theindex.html)
