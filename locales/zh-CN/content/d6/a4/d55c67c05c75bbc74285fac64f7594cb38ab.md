# 简介

Factor 的`calendar`词汇将时间点表示为`timestamp`，将一段时间表示为`duration`。

```factor
USING: calendar ;

2024 7 14 <date> .          ! a timestamp at midnight on 2024-07-14
2024 7 14 <date> >date< .   ! => 14   (2024 and 7 below it)
```

`day-of-week`对星期日返回`0`，对星期六返回`6`；`leap-year?`和`days-in-month`回答常见的日历问题：

```factor
2024 1 8 <date> day-of-week .          ! => 1
2024 leap-year? .                      ! => t
2024 2 1 <date> days-in-month .        ! => 29
```

像`days`和`weeks`这样的时长会用`time+`移动时间戳：

```factor
2024 2 28 <date> 1 days time+ >date< .
! => 29   (2024-02-29)
```
