# 推荐的学习资源

[Delphi Basics](http://www.delphibasics.co.uk/)是关于 Delphi 的一份很好的参考资料。

我也推荐阅读 [delphi.about.com](http://delphi.about.com/) 上的“[A Beginner's Guide to Delphi Programming](http://delphi.about.com/od/beginners/a/delphicourse.htm)”，它是一份不错的 Delphi 编程入门读物。

以下主题来自前面提到的入门指南，应该会对你有所帮助：

1. [字符串处理例程](http://delphi.about.com/od/beginners/fl/String-Handling-Routines-Delphi-Programming.htm)
1. [序数数据类型](http://delphi.about.com/od/faqstipstricks/fl/Ordinal-Data-Types-in-Delphi.htm)
1. [从函数返回多个值](http://delphi.about.com/od/beginners/a/return-multiple-values-from-a-delphi-function.htm)
1. [重载与默认参数](http://delphi.about.com/od/objectpascalide/a/overloading.htm)
1. [类助手与记录助手](http://delphi.about.com/od/oopindelphi/a/understanding-delphi-class-and-record-helpers.htm)
1. [用于集合和其他简单类型的记录助手](http://delphi.about.com/od/objectpascalide/a/delphi-record-helpers-for-sets-and-other-simple-types.htm)
1. [使用 TDictionary 哈希表](http://delphi.about.com/od/beginners/a/using-t-dictionary-hash-tables-in-delphi.htm)
1. [理解泛型类型](http://delphi.about.com/od/objectpascalide/a/understanding-generic-types-in-delphi.htm)
1. [接口](http://delphi.about.com/od/oopindelphi/a/interfaces-in-delphi-programming-101.htm)
1. [处理异常](http://delphi.about.com/od/objectpascalide/a/on-handling-exceptions-in-delphi-exception-handling.htm)
1. [并行编程库教程](http://docwiki.embarcadero.com/RADStudio/Tokyo/en/Parallel_Programming_Library_Tutorials)
