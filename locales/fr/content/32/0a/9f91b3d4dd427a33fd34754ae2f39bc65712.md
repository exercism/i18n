## Astuces pour `jq`

L'expression [`if-then-else`][if] te sera utile dans cet exercice.

Un exemple :

```jq
8, 10, 12 | if . < 10 then "\(.) is less than ten"
            elif . > 10 then "\(.) is more than ten"
            else "\(.) equals ten"
            end
```

affiche

```json
"8 is less than ten"
"10 equals ten"
"12 is more than ten"
```

[if]: https://jqlang.org/manual/#if-then-else-end
