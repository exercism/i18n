* [CLI](https://docs.raku.org/language/create-cli)
* [Operatori](https://docs.raku.org/language/operators)
* [Concorrenza](https://docs.raku.org/language/concurrency)
* [Grammatiche](https://docs.raku.org/language/grammar_tutorial)

* [Funzioni](https://docs.raku.org/language/functions)
* [Espressioni regolari](https://docs.raku.org/language/regexes-best-practices)
