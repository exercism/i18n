# Informazioni

C# ha degli operatori integrati per le comuni operazioni matematiche:

- [`+`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#addition-operator-): addizione
- [`-`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#subtraction-operator--): sottrazione
- [`/`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#division-operator-): divisione
- [`*`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#multiplication-operator-): moltiplicazione
- [`%`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#remainder-operator-): resto

Ci sono anche due operatori speciali:

- [`++`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#increment-operator-): incremento
- [`--`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#decrement-operator---): decremento

Questi operatori aggiungono o sottraggono uno a un valore.
