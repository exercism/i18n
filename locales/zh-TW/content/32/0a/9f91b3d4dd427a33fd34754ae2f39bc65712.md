# 佔位符

## `jq` 小技巧

[`if-then-else` 運算式][if]在這個練習中會很有幫助。

範例：

```jq
8, 10, 12 | if . < 10 then "\(.) is less than ten"
            elif . > 10 then "\(.) is more than ten"
            else "\(.) equals ten"
            end
```

輸出：

```json
"8 is less than ten"
"10 equals ten"
"12 is more than ten"
```

[if]: https://jqlang.org/manual/#if-then-else-end
