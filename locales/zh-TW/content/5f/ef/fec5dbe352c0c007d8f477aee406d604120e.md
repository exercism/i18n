# 關於

C# 內建了常見數學運算的運算子：

- [`+`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#addition-operator-)：加法
- [`-`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#subtraction-operator--)：減法
- [`/`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#division-operator-)：除法
- [`*`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#multiplication-operator-)：乘法
- [`%`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#remainder-operator-)：取餘數

另外還有兩個特殊的運算子：

- [`++`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#increment-operator-)：遞增
- [`--`](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/operators/arithmetic-operators#decrement-operator---)：遞減

這兩個運算子分別會將值加一或減一。
