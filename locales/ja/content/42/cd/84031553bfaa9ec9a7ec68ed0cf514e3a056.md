```text
  a b c d e f g h

6 _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ 6
5 _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ 5
4 _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ 4
3 _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ 3
2 _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ 2
1 _ _ _ W _ _ _ _ 1
  a b c d e f g h
```
