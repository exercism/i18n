## `jq` tippek

Az [`if-then-else` kifejezés][if] hasznos lesz ebben a feladatban.

Egy példa:

```jq
8, 10, 12 | if . < 10 then "\(.) is less than ten"
            elif . > 10 then "\(.) is more than ten"
            else "\(.) equals ten"
            end
```

Kimenete:

```json
"8 is less than ten"
"10 equals ten"
"12 is more than ten"
```

[if]: https://jqlang.org/manual/#if-then-else-end
